Dairy

The dairy producer built the scene, supplied the brief interpretation and replayed its own visual opinions through the optional adapter. It also planted the fourteen test faults in copies of its finished scene. This is not independent grading.

Preparation proposed 37 requirements: three script-only, 24 visual, two both, and eight unsupported. The proposed mapping and tolerances remained pending. A caller-authored map added 114 measurements: selected bounds, clearances, bindings, textures and 63 pipe-to-port pairs. Those measurements do not establish valid process topology, acceptable plant operation or approval of the agent-chosen tolerances.

The fourteen seeded faults yielded two baseline rejections and seven with mapped checks. A blocked aisle escaped the selected scripts. The control results include the unchanged control separately. They describe coverage of deliberately chosen cases, not a general producer failure rate.

The dedicated separator view clipped its sign and process board. Interactive navigation remained untested. Successful content checks used the folder-based USD package after the separate USDZ encountered an input-admission error. The original failing binding revision was not preserved. Broader acceptance remained NEEDS_REVIEW.

Review arrangements

The dairy producer used Claude in Cowork. The distribution-centre producer used gpt-6-astra/xhigh in a fresh project. Its coordinator wrote the brief and prepared the checks before delivery. It had seen portions of draft code, and the roles shared infrastructure. No findings went back to the builder before its first complete delivery was preserved.

Separate review agents recorded initial findings before seeing scripted verdicts, with some prior topic-instruction background disclosed in the original records. The larger trial did not run the harness’s runtime LLM/VLM judge adapter. These arrangements and differing tasks do not support a model ranking or a blinded comparison.

Neither case establishes contact physics, dairy treatment performance, facility throughput, engineering qualification, general defect-detection rates, time savings or final owner acceptance.
