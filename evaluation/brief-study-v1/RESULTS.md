# Brief inputs: evidence and replay

The retained-panel examples demonstrate five assessments of unchanged scene bytes: no brief, size only, matching image, changed image and an explicitly recorded conflict. `reproduce.py` runs these without a model. Outcomes are general acceptance, acceptance within the size scope, acceptance within the matching-image scope, rejection for the changed pixels, and required review for conflicting instructions. These are assistant-authored sample requirements, not independent human instructions.

The private creation study additionally completed three fresh simple-prompt deliveries and three deliveries given a detailed text/image brief. The seven frozen requirement checks passed 0/7 in each simple-prompt delivery and 7/7, 7/7 and 6/7 in the detailed deliveries. The simple producer had not received those detailed targets, so disagreement is not an instruction-following failure. All six deliveries had at least one failure under the selected default delivery profile. The last detailed delivery reversed the reference image, disagreeing at all 4,096 pixels, despite claiming that it matched. No scene faults or repairs were introduced by the evaluator.

Two missing mappings for requirements already present in the brief were added after generation began: explicitly authored coordinate metadata and exact clock endpoints. Original seven-check outcomes were preserved. Those supplemental measurements must not be represented as preregistered. Assembly-part presence, visual quality, surface appearance and continuous motion still need separate evidence/review.

The study used bounded, tool-disabled USD authoring with a fixed raster adapter. It did not recreate the full tool-using demo applications. Three repetitions per arm cannot establish a general failure rate, and the combined text/image creation condition does not isolate the benefit of the image. Optional model reviews were advisory and saw script findings, so agreement was not independent rediscovery. A first review was excluded because its diagnostic view used the wrong display-axis convention; its raw evidence was preserved.

The owner's private evidence packet is `Agentic 3D Lifecycle/experiments/harness-brief-study-20261002/` in the Learning workspace. It contains the frozen protocol and inputs, all model outputs and failed launches, unchanged deliveries, original and amended evaluations, diagnostic views, optional reviews and the linked comparison report. This source checkout does not package those private run records. No publication or model call occurs in `reproduce.py`.

See [the input and adapter guide](../../docs/BRIEFS_AND_MODEL_REVIEW.md) for runnable examples and extension boundaries. `prepare.py` builds the examples, `run_saved.py` evaluates the retained panel, `run_producers.py` explicitly invokes six producer calls, `evaluate_producers.py` evaluates saved deliveries, and `build_report.py` renders the study packet. Live generation is a separate, opt-in operation.

Installed-wheel verification passed 410 tests without skips, 22 provider-pack cases, 32 mesh cases, 17 timing cases, 21 consolidation cases, four reader-report examples, five brief examples, four content probes and four recorded simulator runs. Earlier outcomes were preserved. The main and extension-example wheels were newly installed without editable source; already-installed pinned dependencies were reused. Two usable live advisory reviews are retained separately from these model-free software checks.
