## What to measure

Check source immutability and script per-check outcome parity with the original full-target report. Count completed reviews, runtime failures, effective consistent/concern/unknown items, opinion changes on the three repeats and the evidence used by every concern. Compare general no-brief and supplied-full-target review on the same scene. Explicitly label the script-aware probe; its agreement cannot count as independent rediscovery.

A model-only concern is initially an unverified advisory claim. It becomes a supported added finding only after retained scene evidence or an independent measurement corroborates it. Record false alarms and unresolved concerns, without replacing the model response. Known IDs/hashes prove traceability, not semantic correctness. The same model family and same-assistant rubric are not independent validation. No calibrated precision/recall or saved review-time claim is possible without independently reviewed ground truth and a suitable benchmark.

This study checks the working integration and provides examples of bounded review. It does not establish rendered material correctness, continuous motion, collision safety, physical validity, or model superiority. If insufficient views prevent a useful judgment, preserve that negative result instead of inventing visual evidence.
