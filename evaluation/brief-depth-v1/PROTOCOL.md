# Brief depth across complex scenes — frozen study protocol

October 3, 2026. Owner requested more fresh scene creation with simple prompts and light, medium and detailed briefs. Private exploratory evidence; no publication.

## Design fixed before generation

Three independent synthetic task families: a gantry inspection cell, a rotary indexing bench, and a pump/valve skid. Four creation conditions: simple prompt, light brief, medium brief, detailed brief. Two fresh-context repetitions per task/condition: **24 trial slots**. These are multi-part illustrations with at least 40 visible geometry prims requested, not calibrated machines or the earlier demo applications. No private demo assets are used.

All conditions receive identical output/naming/admission conventions, including Z-up metre coordinates, a complete USDA string, an 8×8 palette raster and notes. The simple condition is not an absence of instructions. Light adds platform dimensions/centre and repeated-part count. Medium adds a directed cabinet gap, moving-part size, exact clock/range and two endpoint positions. Detailed adds a 21-position trajectory and an exact supplied image. The additional image occurs only at detailed depth, so this tests a bundled increase in information; it cannot isolate word count, image benefit, tolerance presentation or any single prompt feature.

The ten full-target check IDs are fixed in preparation. The selected numerical check counts available in the producer's own instructions are 1, 3, 8 and 10. These counts are comparisons, not independent assets. Endpoint checks and trajectory checks overlap. Qualitative completeness, visible component count, connected appearance, full continuous motion and physical behavior remain explicitly outside the measured guarantee. The default diagnostics may cover parts of these areas but do not establish complete intent coverage.

## Three assessments per delivered scene

1. The unchanged 27-check general delivery baseline, without a brief.
2. General checks plus the mapped requirements actually supplied to that producer. This measures compliance within those disclosed targets.
3. General checks plus the same full target brief for every condition of the task. This measures alignment with the final target. Failure on a target never supplied to the producer is **not** an instruction-following failure.

Record every PASS, FAIL, UNKNOWN, ERROR, warning, skipped subject and unassessed trial. Primary tables include all 24 scheduled slots, with missing deliveries shown explicitly rather than discarded or assigned fabricated measurements. Preserve per-check subject units/counts and observed/expected values. Report individual results and task/condition summaries; do not pool subject counts into a quality score or infer a population failure rate.

## Generation

Request `gpt-6-astra`, high reasoning, through the already available bundled Codex CLI. Record executable/version, exact prompts and images, requested model, available usage, elapsed time and native events. Resolved model snapshot, seed and sampling settings are unavailable. Each call uses a fresh ephemeral context, blank working directory and disabled tools/apps/plugins/multi-agent features. No evaluator result, previous response or repair feedback enters a creation call.

A fixed seed determines the scheduling order: shuffle task/repetition blocks and the four conditions within each block. Use at most two concurrent calls, chosen before generation; retain actual start times and do not claim controlled latency/cost comparisons. Per-call deadline 900 seconds and native output budget 8 MiB. One trial directory is reserved per slot. Preserve all partial output and failures. Do not rerun content failures, invalid deliveries or partial-output timeouts. A clearly pre-response transient infrastructure failure may have one separately recorded retry with identical inputs; no successful/partial response can be replaced. Authentication or model-availability failures require a documented infrastructure decision before continuing.

The adapter writes model USDA verbatim and expands each model-provided palette cell to an 8×8 pixel block in a 64×64 PNG. It does not repair geometry, metadata, materials, animation or pixels. Only after generation are frozen assessment brief files copied beside the saved delivery. No producer-authored executable code is run.

## Evaluator controls and freeze

Before the first call, verify each task's positive fixture passes all ten mapped full-target checks. Independently mutate size, repeated count, gap, moving-part size, clock, time range, endpoint, interior trajectory, image pixels and coordinate metadata; each intended check must detect its perturbation. These scripted controls are evaluator tests, not model failures. Include an endpoint-preserving interior-motion mutation: endpoints should pass while the denser trajectory fails. Include an RGB image mutation that remains readable under default diagnostics but fails reference pixels.

Freeze protocol, task definitions, prompts, source images, check maps, runner and installed checker identity before production. Retain original results if an evaluator defect is discovered; write an amendment and show reruns separately. Never edit production scenes to satisfy the checks. Diagnostic projections can support browsing, but do not prove texture mapping, visibility of all parts, engineering validity or physical behavior. No model judge is required for the primary numerical comparison.

## Interpretation

Separate defects against supplied requirements, differences from unprovided final targets, general delivery-profile findings and coverage limitations. Six deliveries per condition across three tasks is a small, correlated exploratory sample, with only two repeats per task/condition. Requirements and maps are assistant-authored at the owner's request, not independently collected human briefs or an external validation benchmark. Preserve disconfirming cases: a more detailed brief may not monotonically improve results, and a simple prompt may match targets by chance. Evidence of detection on these checks does not establish detection of every scene problem or time saved by using the harness.
